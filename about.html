Biography

Avijit Mukul Kishore

Avijit Mukul Kishore is a filmmaker and cinematographer based in Mumbai. He works primarily in documentary and inter-disciplinary moving-image practice. He has made eleven documentary films as director and worked on several others as cinematographer. He has shot one fiction feature and worked on television talk shows and advertising films.

Kishore has been involved in cinema pedagogy as a lecturer and curator of film programmes for over two decades. He has worked with state film and media archives and is interested in the non-fiction film form and moving-image history.

Before directing, he worked as a broadcaster on state radio and television for five years and was a member of two theatre groups in Delhi.
