Filmography as Director

Directed Works

An overview of twelve directed documentary films exploring themes of identity, modern architecture, memory, queer voices, and artistic transitions in post-colonial India.

"A Minuscule Minority" — Queer Voices in Indian Politics

2025 74 min 4K Video Eng, Hin, Mar

A documentary on queer voices and their relationship to Indian electoral politics.

Screenings International Documentary and Short Film Festival of Kerala (IDSFFK), Trivandrum

An Election Diary

2023 32 min 4K Video Hindi

A documentary filmed during state assembly elections in northern India.

Screenings Centre for Modern Indian Studies, University of Gottingen, Germany · IDSFFK Kerala, Trivandrum

Lovely Villa - Architecture as Autobiography

2019 32 min HD Video Co-Directed with Rohan Shivkumar

A film on a modernist house in suburban Mumbai, tracing its design and the family memories associated with it. Co-directed with Rohan Shivkumar.

Screenings Urban Lens Festival (Delhi & Bangalore) · PSBT Open Frame · Kalaghoda Art Festival · Nomad Cafe · Arthshila, Santiniketan

Squeeze Lime in Your Eye

2018 58 min HD Video Eng, Hin, Ben

A documentary on art education and visual practice in India, shot across several art institutions.

Screenings Kochi-Muziris Biennale · Urban Lens Festival · PSBT Open Frame Festival · Chatterjee and Lal Art Gallery · Nomad Cafe

The Garden of Forgotten Snow

2017 30 min Best Experimental Doc

An experimental film on art, identity and landscape. Sits between documentary and visual art practice.

Accolades Best Experimental Documentary (Signs Film Festival, Thrissur) · Documenta Madrid · Kochi-Muziris Biennale · Films on Art Portugal, Lisbon · IDSFFK Kerala · Gallery Chemould

Nostalgia for the Future

2017 54 min HD Video & 16mm Film Best Documentary Award

A documentary on the architecture of the modern Indian state — planned cities and institutional buildings. Co-directed with Rohan Shivkumar.

Accolades Best Documentary Award (IDSFFK Kerala) · Jury's Special Mention (Signs Film Festival) · Sharjah Architecture Triennial · Chicago Architecture Biennial · Pinakothek Der Moderne, Munich · Documenta 14

Electric Shadows — Journeys in Image-Making

2016 45 min HD Video Eng, Hin, Man

A documentary on the history of cinema projection and image-making, tracing practitioners across India and China.

Screenings Urban Lens Festival, Bangalore · PSBT Open Frame, Delhi · National Film Archive of India (NFAI), Pune

Jaya He New Museum — Art Project

2015 Multi-part series 10 Short Films

A multi-part documentary project made for the GVK Museum at Mumbai Airport Terminal 2.

Context Permanent museum video installations and archive documentaries for the GVK Jaya He Airport Museum.

To Let the World In (Volume I & II)

2012–2013 93 & 53 min Audience Prize

A two-volume documentary on three generations of modern and contemporary visual art practice in India.

Accolades Audience Prize (Sheffield Doc/fest, UK) · APSA Nominated (Melbourne) · Mumbai International Film Festival · Tempo Film Festival, Stockholm · Asia Art Archive, Hong Kong · NGMA Mumbai

Vertical City

2011 34 min HD Video Hindi, English Special Jury Mentions

A documentary on Mumbai's slum rehabilitation programme and the conditions of life it produces in high-rise towers.

Accolades & Screenings Special Jury Mention (IDSFFK Kerala) · Special Jury Mention (London International Doc Festival) · Melbourne International Film Festival · CPH DOX, Copenhagen · Sheffield Doc/fest · Dok-Leipzig · IFFI Goa · INIVA London · Experimenta India · PSBT Open Frame, Delhi · VIBGYOR Film Festival, Thrissur (Closing Film) · Signs Film Festival, Trivandrum · French Embassy Cultural Centres (Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Chandigarh, Bangalore, Chennai, Goa)

Certified Universal

2009 15 min HD Video Hindi, Marathi, English

A short documentary on film certification and public exhibition in India. Selected for Forum Expanded, Berlinale.

Screenings Forum Expanded, Berlinale · National Gallery of Modern Art (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore) · IDSFFK Kerala, Trivandrum · Indo American Arts Council, New York · Prague Indian Film Festival · Kala Ghoda Art Festival, Mumbai · National Centre for the Performing Arts, Mumbai

Snapshots from a Family Album

2004 63 min Digital Video Hindi, English

A documentary tracing personal and social histories through family photographs.

Screenings Hong Kong Arts Centre · Korean National University of Arts, Seoul · Kino Arsenal & Deutsche Guggenheim, Berlin · Persisting Visions (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Kunming, Hong Kong) · Bangalore Queer Film Festival · Sommerszene Vienna · Video as Urban Condition Project, Linz · Lille 3000, Lille · Amsterdam India Festival · Film South Asia, Kathmandu · Cinema Nova, Brussels · NFAI Pune · Prague India Film Festival · Bollywood and Beyond, Stuttgart · Experimenta India · NCPA Mumbai
