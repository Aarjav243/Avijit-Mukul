Snapshots from a Family Album

An early feature-length documentary tracing personal and collective memory through the photographic archive.

Snapshots from a Family Album is Avijit Mukul Kishore's debut feature-length documentary — a 63-minute work that uses family photographs as a portal into personal memory, collective identity, and the histories that ordinary people carry within them.

The film screened extensively across Asia, Europe, and the Americas — from Hong Kong and Seoul to Berlin, Brussels, Vienna, and Kathmandu — establishing Kishore's practice in international documentary circuits from the outset.
