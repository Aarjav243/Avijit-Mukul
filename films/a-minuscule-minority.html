"A Minuscule Minority"
Queer Voices in Indian Politics

An intimate inquiry into queer representation, citizenship, and political movements in Indian democracy.

"A Minuscule Minority" is a documentary on queer communities and their relationship to Indian electoral politics. The film brings together voices from activists, politicians, lawyers and citizens.

It was made following the decriminalisation of Section 377 and looks at how LGBTQ+ communities engage with the state and public life in contemporary India.
