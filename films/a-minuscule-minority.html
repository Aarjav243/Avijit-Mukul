"A Minuscule Minority"
Queer Voices in Indian Politics

An intimate inquiry into queer representation, citizenship, and political movements in Indian democracy.

"A Minuscule Minority" is a feature-length documentary engaging with the landscape of queer politics in India — examining how LGBTQ+ communities navigate the state, legal frameworks, electoral participation, and public life in contemporary India.

Filmed in 4K video across multiple locations, the film brings together diverse voices — activists, politicians, lawyers, and citizens — to map the evolving terrain of queer rights and recognition after the historic decriminalisation of Section 377.
