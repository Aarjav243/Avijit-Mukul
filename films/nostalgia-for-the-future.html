Nostalgia for the Future

An architectural and political exploration of Indian modernities — planned cities, state architectures, and the utopias that never arrived.

Nostalgia for the Future, co-directed with Rohan Shivkumar, examines the architecture of the modern Indian nation-state — its planned cities, institutional buildings, and utopian spatial imaginaries. Shot on HD video and 16mm film, the film blends archival material with contemporary observation.

The film was shown at Documenta 14 in Athens, the Sharjah Architecture Triennial, and the Chicago Architecture Biennial — establishing Kishore's practice at the intersection of documentary cinema and the visual arts internationally.
