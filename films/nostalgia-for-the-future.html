Nostalgia for the Future

An architectural and political exploration of Indian modernities — planned cities, state architectures, and the utopias that never arrived.

Nostalgia for the Future, co-directed with Rohan Shivkumar, looks at the architecture of the modern Indian state — planned cities, institutional buildings and the spatial ambitions of the post-independence period. It was shot on HD video and 16mm film.

The film was shown at Documenta 14 in Athens, the Sharjah Architecture Triennial and the Chicago Architecture Biennial, and received the Best Documentary Award at IDSFFK Kerala.
