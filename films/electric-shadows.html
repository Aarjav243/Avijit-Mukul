Electric Shadows
Journeys in Image-Making

A documentary tracing the history of cinema technologies, projection practices, and the moving image across India and China.

Electric Shadows: Journeys in Image-Making is a documentary exploration of cinema history — tracing the lives of projectionists, archivists, and film practitioners across India and China, linking the material culture of image-making with personal and collective memory.

Shot in three languages — English, Hindi, and Mandarin — the film moves between continents to ask what it means to dedicate a life to moving images, and what is lost when projection technologies and the cultures around them disappear.
