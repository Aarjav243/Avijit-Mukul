Electric Shadows
Journeys in Image-Making

A documentary tracing the history of cinema technologies, projection practices, and the moving image across India and China.

Electric Shadows: Journeys in Image-Making is a documentary on the history of cinema projection and image-making, tracing practitioners across India and China. It was shot in English, Hindi and Mandarin.

The film looks at projectionists, archivists and filmmakers, and the technologies and practices around the moving image in both countries.
