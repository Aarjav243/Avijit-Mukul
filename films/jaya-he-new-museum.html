Jaya He New Museum
Art Project at Mumbai Airport Terminal 2

A multi-part film project documenting the GVK Museum at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport, Mumbai.

Jaya He New Museum is an extensive multi-part documentary project created for the GVK Museum integrated within Mumbai Airport Terminal 2. Comprising 45-minute and 25-minute versions alongside 10 individual short films, the project documents the artworks, artists, and curatorial vision that shaped one of India's largest public art museums.

The project stands as a significant commission — bridging documentary filmmaking, institutional history, and the public life of contemporary Indian art.
