An Election Diary

A documentary observation of localized electoral dynamics and public discourse during state assemblies in Northern India.

An Election Diary is a 32-minute documentary filmed during state assembly elections in northern India. It observes the campaign trail, polling booths and conversations at the margins of the formal electoral process.

The film was screened at the Centre for Modern Indian Studies, University of Göttingen, and at IDSFFK Kerala.
