Certified Universal

A short documentary on moving-image culture, film censorship, and public exhibition in India — selected for Forum Expanded at the Berlinale.

Certified Universal is a 15-minute documentary on film certification in India and its relationship to questions of public exhibition and cultural expression.

The film was selected for Forum Expanded at the Berlinale and was also shown at the National Gallery of Modern Art in Mumbai, Delhi and Bangalore.
