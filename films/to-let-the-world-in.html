To Let the World In
Volume I & II

A landmark two-volume documentary tracking three generations of modern and contemporary visual art practice in India.

Volume I — 93 min

Volume II — 53 min

To Let the World In is a two-volume documentary on three generations of modern and contemporary visual art practice in India. Shot across studios, galleries and archives, the film records conversations with painters, sculptors and installation artists.

Volume I is 93 minutes and Volume II is 53 minutes. The film screened at major international festivals and art institutions, including Sheffield Doc/fest, Mumbai International Film Festival, Asia Art Archive Hong Kong and the National Gallery of Modern Art.
