Squeeze Lime in Your Eye

A film on pedagogy, visual practice, and the training of artists in contemporary India.

Squeeze Lime in Your Eye is a 58-minute documentary that enters the world of art pedagogy in India — observing artists, teachers, and students as they negotiate tradition, experiment, and the pressures of a rapidly changing cultural landscape.

Shot in English, Hindi, and Bengali across multiple art institutions, the film is at once a portrait of specific individuals and a broader meditation on how artistic knowledge is transmitted, contested, and renewed.
