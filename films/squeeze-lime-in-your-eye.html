Squeeze Lime in Your Eye

A film on pedagogy, visual practice, and the training of artists in contemporary India.

Squeeze Lime in Your Eye is a 58-minute documentary on art education in India. Shot across several art institutions, it observes artists, teachers and students.

The film was made in English, Hindi and Bengali and was shown at the Kochi-Muziris Biennale, Urban Lens Festival and Chatterjee and Lal gallery, among other venues.
