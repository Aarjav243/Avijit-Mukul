Vertical City

A political critique of slum rehabilitation in Mumbai — examining spatial control, vertical living, and the design of poverty.

Vertical City is a 34-minute documentary examining Mumbai's slum rehabilitation programme — in which sprawling horizontal settlements are demolished and their residents relocated into high-rise towers. Kishore interrogates the politics of this spatial transformation: who decides where the poor live, what kind of life is produced in these vertical spaces, and what is lost in the transition.

The film was selected for Melbourne International Film Festival, CPH DOX in Copenhagen, Sheffield Doc/fest, and Dok-Leipzig — receiving Special Jury Mentions at both IDSFFK Kerala and the London International Documentary Festival.
