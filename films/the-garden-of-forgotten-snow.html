The Garden of Forgotten Snow

An experimental inquiry into art, identity, and landscape — observing changing spaces and historical reflections.

The Garden of Forgotten Snow is an experimental film that moves between landscape, memory and historical residue. It sits between documentary and visual art practice.

The film was selected for Documenta Madrid and the Kochi-Muziris Biennale, and received the Best Experimental Documentary award at Signs Film Festival, Thrissur.
