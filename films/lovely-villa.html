Lovely Villa
Architecture as Autobiography

An auto-biographical reflection on modernist residential architecture, family memory, and urban aspirations in suburban Mumbai.

Lovely Villa – Architecture as Autobiography is a co-directed film by Avijit Mukul Kishore and Rohan Shivkumar. The film traces a single residential building in suburban Mumbai — its design, its inhabitants, and the memories it holds — as a way of reading broader histories of modernist aspiration and middle-class life in post-independence India.

Architecture becomes a lens through which the filmmakers examine family narratives, spatial imagination, and the ways in which a home carries the weight of an entire generation's hopes and displacements.
