Articles & Papers

‘Cinematic Delhi: Multiple Histories of the Capital in Hindi Films’

An extensive critical investigation into how Delhi's urban development, political shifts, and historical landscapes are curated, narrated, and represented in classic and contemporary mainstream Hindi films.

‘Street Corner, 1985’: Analysis of a painting by Sudhir Patwardhan

An aesthetic and sociological analysis of Sudhir Patwardhan's landmark painting, detailing working-class representation, urban layouts, and post-colonial city planning in Indian fine art.

Moving Image Review 2018

'You've Told Me That Three Times Now: Propaganda / Anti-propaganda in the FD Documentary 1965-75’

Published in the Moving Image Review and Journal (UK), this paper dissects state-sponsored films, archival practices, and the fine line between governmental narrative and independent non-fiction expression inside India's Films Division.

‘Record, Replay: Imaging Technology and the Post-Digital Representation of the Real’

Investigating the philosophical transition from analog film stock to post-digital video capture, exploring how changing technology alters our active understanding of historical reality and documentary evidence.
